# AWS Cloud Practitioner (CLF-C02): study guide

[🇧🇷 Português](README.md) · 🇺🇸 English

A complete study guide for the **AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner (CLF-C02)** certification, written for the final review before the exam. It covers all 4 domains of the official exam guide with comparison tables, question clues, diagrams and the most common traps.

## What's inside

- **27 sections**, from section 0 (how the exam works) to section 26 (service pairs the exam swaps), organized by exam domain.
- **"Clue in the question" tables**: for each service, the words that tend to appear in a question when it is the answer.
- **Trap boxes** wherever the answer options look alike: Multi-AZ vs. Read Replica, CloudTrail vs. Config, SQS vs. SNS, Shield vs. WAF and many more.
- **Mermaid diagrams** (GitHub renders them natively): global infrastructure, three-tier VPC, peering vs. Transit Gateway, layers of protection, analytics pipeline and SNS/SQS fan-out.
- **Support plans updated for 2026**: Business Support+, Enterprise Support and Unified Operations, alongside the legacy plans, which go away on January 1, 2027.
- **Notes on renamed and retired services** (Data Firehose, QLDB, Pinpoint, CodeCommit, the 2025 Free Tier), so old names in practice exams don't throw you off.

## How to study with it

1. **First pass:** read it in order, one section per study session. Domains 2 and 3 are 64% of the exam; give them more time.
2. **Practice exams:** for every question you miss, go back to the matching section and look for the clue you missed.
3. **Day before:** reread only the **Trap** boxes, section 26 and the trigger words table at the end.
